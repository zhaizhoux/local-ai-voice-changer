# Third-party projects

- [Applio](https://github.com/IAHispano/Applio) provides the external RVC inference engine. Its source LICENSE identifies the MIT license and Copyright (c) 2026 AI Hispano. Follow the installed engine's own license and terms.
- [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/) provides the tensor and model runtime through the external engine environment.
- [python-sounddevice](https://python-sounddevice.readthedocs.io/) provides audio device access through PortAudio.
- [VB-CABLE](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) is a separately installed proprietary donationware audio driver.

The release contains the control-panel application source. Engine source, pretrained weights, voice recordings and driver binaries are acquired separately under their respective terms.
